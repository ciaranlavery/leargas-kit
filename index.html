Questions

No new software. Cancel anytime.

Do I need new software or hardware?

No. The pack is built from the systems you already hold — till, bookings, bank, sheets. Nothing to install, nothing to learn.

How do you connect to my till and bank? Is it safe?

Read-only connections where possible — Open Banking for bank feeds, official integrations or exports for till and bookings. No passwords stored, you can revoke access anytime, and we only pull what the Monday pack needs. UK GDPR applies; data stays in the UK/EEA.

How do the £195 extras work?

Each extra answers one money question your accounts can't — did the promo profit, are busy hours covered without idle ones, who's close to buying. Built around your data, one at a time, cancel anytime.

When do I get my first pack?

On the first Monday within 10 days of setup, once everything's connected and checked. You review a single page to start the week.

What does the timeline and billing look like?

Free 30-min chat → £150 one-off setup on video → first pack on the next Monday within 10 days → £595 covers months one and two → then £595 +VAT a month, rolling, cancel with 30 days. Cancel anytime and the dashboard stops with your subscription; any PDFs you've saved stay yours.
